# Wizarding School Game

## Project Overview

A side-scrolling wizarding school adventure game where the player creates a character, prepares for school, and embarks on a magical journey culminating in saving the day.

## Game Flow

1. **Character Creation** - Player creates their wizard character
2. **Shopping for Supplies** - Buy school supplies (wand, books, robes, etc.)
3. **Train to School** - Travel to the wizarding school
4. **House Sorting** - Get sorted into a school house
5. **Making Friends** - Meet and befriend other students
6. **Attending Classes** - Learn spells and magical skills
7. **The Big Problem** - A major threat emerges that the player must resolve

## Technical Stack & Tools

### Character Generation
- **Universal LPC Spritesheet Generator** for character sprite creation and customization

### Movement & Controls
- Side-scrolling gameplay (Hollow Knight style)
- Walk left/right
- Jump
- Cast spells in any direction
- Fly a broomstick

### AI Generation
- **Gemini 2.5 Flash** - Background image generation and item art generation
- **Claude Opus** - Dialogue generation for NPCs, story events, and interactions
- **Important:** AI generations must only be executed when explicitly requested

### Gameplay Mechanics
- **Quick-time mini games** for learning and executing spells
